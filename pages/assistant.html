About the AI and what it sees

To turn AI on: in Vercel → Project → Settings → Environment Variables add ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, ASSISTANT_ALLOWED_EMAILS (your sign-in email) and run supabase/assistant_usage.sql, then redeploy and sign in. AI stays off until all are in place. Each button press with AI on is one paid request (about £0.001 on the default model; see SETUP.md §4).

Sent per request: today's open tasks, today's commitments, at most 3 matching Second Brain excerpts and your question. Not your whole notes collection, finances or anything else.

Without AI you get a basic briefing built only from your stored tasks and commitments.

Briefings run only when you press a button — nothing is scheduled. No email is connected. Nothing is sent, booked or changed outside this app.
